**Bottles is a quiet place for your links, notes and files.**

## No feed, no noise

Most tools for saving things slowly turn into another feed to scroll. Bottles doesn't. There's nothing to keep up with and nothing competing for your attention: just the things you chose to keep.

## Two spaces

One space for what you keep: bookmarks, files and references, filed properly so you can find them again. One space for what you write: notes and docs that live right next to what inspired them.

## Try it

Bottles is live. [Open Bottles](https://bottles.taohq.org) and start filing.
