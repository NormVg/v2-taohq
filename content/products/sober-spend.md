**Sober.Spend is UPI budgeting that happens before you pay, not after: you see what a payment does to your month while you can still change your mind.**

## Check before you pay

Scan a UPI QR inside the app and see the payment's effect on your month and on its category. Then pay, or skip. Confirming opens your own UPI app with the details filled in and logs the expense. Sober.Spend never moves money.

## Your month at a glance

What's left this month, a daily allowance, and how full each category is. A small pixel cat sums it up in one word, from CHILL to BROKE, with a short comment on your spending.

## Plan, track, split

- **Spend list:** plan groceries, refills and gifts with rough prices, then tick them off.
- **Subscriptions:** recurring charges are counted before they're billed, paused ones included.
- **Auto-categories:** expenses sort themselves by payee, business type or name, and your corrections are remembered.
- **Splitting:** equal or custom splits, in groups or one-on-one. Only your share counts against your budget, every change is logged, and only the person who added an expense can edit it. Friends connect through a profile QR that doesn't expose your name, number or UPI ID.

## Private by design

No bank linking. No SMS reading. Sober.Spend only knows what you scan or add, and it works offline, syncing to your account later.
