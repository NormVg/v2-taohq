**Fold is a phone journal for photos, voice notes and quick thoughts, built for overthinkers, photo dumpers, late-night voice noters, and anyone who bought a journal and never opened it.**

## No blank page

Tap to write. Hold to talk. Swipe up to snap. There are no prompts to answer and no empty page staring back at you. Fold meets you wherever the thought is: a sentence, a photo, a voice note, or a 2am ramble.

## A canvas, not a form

Every page is a canvas. Drag photos, songs, GIFs and voice notes anywhere you want. Capture, attach, add music, a GIF, a video, a recording, or a location, and arrange it the way the day actually felt.

## Keep the streak

A streak counter, ink that slowly fills up your month, and an activity view that shows every day you kept. Three gentle nudges a day (morning, afternoon and evening) open straight into the camera, so writing something down takes a second.

## Yours, and only yours

No account. No cloud. No ads. Your journal lives on your phone and nowhere else.

## Get in early

Fold is free and coming to iOS and Android in 2026. It opens in waves: [join the waitlist](https://fold.taohq.org/#join) to save your spot, and you'll get one email when your invite is ready.
